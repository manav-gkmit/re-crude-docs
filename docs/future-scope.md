# Future Scope

This document outlines the **future enhancements and potential features** for the Re:Crude recruitment automation platform. These improvements aim to make the platform more intelligent, collaborative, and insightful.

---

## Planned Enhancements

### 1. Interview Scheduling
- Integrate with **Calendly** or **Calendar APIs** to automate interview scheduling. 

- Enable automatic slot suggestions based on interviewer availability. 

- Reduce manual coordination and back-and-forth emails.

---

### 2. Multi-Reviewer Consensus
- Support **collaborative decision-making** by aggregating feedback from multiple reviewers. 

- Implement consensus-based candidate recommendations. 

- Ensure fairer and more balanced evaluation outcomes.

---

### 3. Resume Scoring Models
- Introduce **quantitative scoring** of resumes using AI/ML models. 

- Score candidates based on skills, experience, and role alignment. 

- Enable recruiters to prioritize high-potential candidates efficiently.

---

### 4. Hiring Analytics Dashboard
- Provide a **visual dashboard** for recruiters and hiring managers. 

- Track metrics such as: 

  - Candidate pipeline 

  - Interview completion rates 

  - Average time-to-hire 

  - Conversion rates per stage 

- Support data-driven decision-making and process optimization.

---

### 5. Fine-Tuned LLMs per Domain
- Develop **domain-specific LLMs** for more accurate resume parsing and recommendations. 

- Fine-tune models for technical, marketing, finance, and other specialized roles. 

- Improve precision in skill extraction, role fit scoring, and feedback summarization.

---

By implementing these enhancements, Re:Crude can evolve into a **smarter, more collaborative, and insight-driven recruitment platform**, capable of handling complex enterprise hiring needs efficiently.
