# Interview Feedback Reminder Service

This service acts as a gentle nudge to ensure that the interview process keeps moving forward. It tracks pending feedback and sends reminders to interviewers, helping to avoid bottlenecks and delays.

## Core Responsibilities

- **Track Pending Feedback:** The service maintains a record of all outstanding feedback requests, including who needs to provide feedback and for which candidate.

- **Send Scheduled Reminders:** If feedback is not submitted within a predefined timeframe, the service will automatically send a reminder to the interviewer. The reminders can be configured to be sent at different intervals (e.g., after 24 hours, 48 hours).

- **Escalate Unresolved Feedback:** If feedback is still not submitted after multiple reminders, the service can escalate the issue. This might involve:
    - Notifying the hiring manager or recruiter.
    - Sending a high-priority notification to the interviewer.
    - Flagging the candidate in the system for manual follow-up.

## The Importance of Timely Feedback

> "A delayed hiring process can lead to losing top candidates to competitors. This service is a simple but powerful tool to help us maintain momentum and provide a better candidate experience."
