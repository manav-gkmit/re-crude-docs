# Interview Feedback Reminder Service

This document describes the **Interview Feedback Reminder Service** in Re:Crude, which automates the process of tracking and collecting interview feedback from reviewers.

---

## Responsibilities

The Interview Feedback Reminder Service is responsible for:

- **Track Pending Feedback**  
  Continuously monitor which interviews have pending feedback and maintain a record of outstanding tasks.  

- **Send Scheduled Reminders**  
  Automatically send reminders via Slack or Email to interviewers at predefined intervals to ensure timely feedback submission.  

- **Escalate Unresolved Feedback**  
  Notify supervisors or hiring managers if feedback remains unresolved beyond a certain threshold, ensuring accountability and completion.

---

This service ensures that the Re:Crude platform maintains **consistent, timely, and complete interview feedback**, improving both recruiter efficiency and the candidate evaluation process.
