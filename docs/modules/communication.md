# Slack & Email Communication Service

This document describes the **Slack & Email Communication Service** in Re:Crude, which manages notifications, updates, and interactions with recruiters and interviewers.

---

## Responsibilities

The Slack & Email Communication Service is responsible for:

- **Send Resume Summaries**  
  Automatically deliver AI-generated resume summaries and candidate recommendations to reviewers and hiring managers via Slack or Email.  

- **Maintain Slack Thread Context**  
  Ensure that all communications related to a candidate are grouped in a single Slack thread to maintain conversation history and context.  

- **Capture Reactions and Replies**  
  Monitor Slack reactions (emojis) and replies to track reviewer decisions or acknowledgments.  

---

## Key Benefits

- Streamlines communication between recruiters and interviewers.  
- Reduces manual follow-ups and ensures timely notifications.  
- Maintains a clear and organized conversation history for auditing and review.  
- Enables actionable insights by capturing reviewer responses directly from Slack and Email.

---

By implementing this service, Re:Crude ensures **efficient, organized, and trackable communication** throughout the recruitment workflow.
